Privacy Policy

Fluff & Fancy Cat Salon

Support Privacy Policy

The short version

We don't collect any information about you or your child. The game has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What the game stores

Your game progress (coins, levels, stickers, furniture, settings and so on) is saved only on your iPad. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes this information.

Pictures

If you choose a photo for your shop cats, the game keeps a copy only on your device. If you save a Photo Booth picture, the game asks for permission first and then adds the picture to your Photos library. The game does not upload, share or look through your photos.

Purchases

The optional Cat Pass is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Purchases are protected by a grown-up check inside the game. Apple's own privacy policy covers App Store purchases.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect names, email addresses, locations or any other personal information.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or third-party services in the game.
  • We don't sell or share any data — there isn't any to share.

Children's privacy

Fluff & Fancy Cat Salon is made for kids and families. Because the game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

If anything changes

If we ever change how the game handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change happens.

❓ Common questions

I bought the Cat Pass, but it's locked on my other iPad.

Open the game, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the iPad is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought the Cat Pass (or one in the same Family Sharing group).

Can my family share the Cat Pass?

Yes! The Cat Pass works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your Apple family group can use it without buying it again.

Will my progress move to a new iPad?

Your shop, coins and stickers are saved on the iPad you play on. If you delete the game, that progress is deleted too. The Cat Pass is never lost — just use Restore Purchases.

How do I start over?

Go to Settings and tap Start over from Day 1. The game asks twice, just to be safe. Your Cat Pass stays unlocked.

How do I turn the music or sounds off?

Tap the round music note in the bottom-left corner to turn the music on or off. You'll find more sound options, and a choice of title songs, in Settings.

Can I make the game easier or harder?

Yes. In Settings, Time Difficulty changes how patient the customer cats are. Each mini game also has Easy, Normal and Hard levels.
